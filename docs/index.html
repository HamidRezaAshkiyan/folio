Designed for Analog + Digital

        
Everything you need for Bullet Journaling, nothing in the way.

        

          Capture thoughts in seconds from your terminal or browser, keep everything as durable local Markdown, and transcribe back to your paper notebook when you sit down at your desk.
        

      


      

        

          

            DUAL INTERFACE
            1 bin
          

          
TUI, Web GUI & Shell CLI

          

            Run folio --tui for a 6-pane Ratatui terminal workspace, folio --gui for a zero-dependency local Web UI (Dark & Light), or folio log "..." for instant shell capture.
          

        


        

          

            CARRY TO PAPER
            [paper:no]
          

          
Built for Physical Notebooks

          

            Every digital entry tracks whether you've copied it into your physical notebook ([paper:no] → [paper:ok]) alongside your current physical page number (Notebook p. 42).
          

        


        

          

            OBSIDIAN MARKDOWN
            [[vault]]
          

          
Plain Local .md Files

          

            No proprietary database or cloud lock-in. Entries live as clean Markdown with YAML frontmatter, [[wikilinks]], and #tags directly inside your Obsidian vault.
          

        


        

          

            CUSTOM SPACES
            Collections
          

          
Organize by Context

          

            Separate Daily Logs from custom Spaces like Studio & Work, Reading & Study, or Personal. Migrate tasks between Tomorrow, any Space, or your Future Log.
          

        


        

          

            REFLECTION
            Reviews
          

          
Weekly, Monthly & Future Logs

          

            Generate structured Weekly (Reviews/YYYY-Www.md) and Monthly reviews automatically, maintain a Future Log.md, and get an auto-updated Monthly Index.
          

        


        

          

            PORTABILITY
            Import/Export
          

          
Bring Your Notes With You

          

            Import from Markdown, Obsidian, Apple Notes, Notion, CSV, JSON, or plain text via folio import. Export any document to Markdown, JSON, or CSV anytime.

Workflow Reference

        
Fast from the shell. Fluid in the TUI.

        

          Log without leaving your terminal, or open the full interactive TUI / Web GUI when planning your day.
        

      


      

        
        

          

            CLI QUICK START
            folio --help
          

          

            
# Launch the Terminal UI (default)
folio
folio --tui

# Launch the embedded Web GUI in your browser
folio --gui
folio --gui --port 3420 --no-open

# Use a custom Obsidian vault path
folio --vault ~/Obsidian/My-Vault --tui

# One-line rapid logging from any shell
folio log ". Finalize studio lighting sketch"
folio log "o Coffee with Maya at Blue Bottle"
folio log "= Calm after walking without headphones"
folio log "- Small repairs made daily"

# Log directly into a custom Space
folio log -s "Reading & Study" "- Finish ch. 4"

# Generate Weekly or Monthly Review
folio review weekly
folio review monthly

# Import / Export notes
folio import ~/Desktop/apple-notes.txt
folio export --format json --output today.json

Plain Text Forever

        
Your vault, readable in Obsidian or any text editor.

        

          Default vault lives at ~/Obsidian/BuJo-Vault — configurable via --vault, FOLIO_VAULT, or ~/.config/folio/config.toml.
